Research projects

MemInject: red-teaming toolkit for memory-augmented LLM agents

2026, ongoing

A PyTorch library that tests how memory-augmented LLM agents hold up against delayed indirect prompt injection. It implements three injection strategies (direct write, semantic blend, retrieval bias) and a benchmark suite. On a mock memory agent, direct injection triggered 75% of the time and semantic blend 67%. Evaluation on production-grade agents is in progress.

Activation steering for deceptive alignment

2026, in progress

An experimental pipeline to detect and suppress deceptive alignment in cooperative multi-agent LLM systems using representation engineering: layer-wise linear probing for a deception-related direction, direction learning, and inference-time steering. Validated end to end on a synthetic testbed; evaluation on a trained multi-agent system is next.

Cross-robustness of lightweight vision systems

2025, completed

A comparison of adversarial robustness (PGD, FGSM) and natural out-of-distribution corruption in lightweight vision models. Quantized models lost 14% robust accuracy, against 5% at full precision, which matters for deployment on constrained hardware.
